## Brand & Style

The design system blends a half-century of neighborhood heritage with crisp, modern digital retail. It captures the sensory ritual of cured calabash gourds, hand-chiseled alpaca silver bombillas, and botanic botanical fragrances, presenting them through an editorial storefront that feels as enduring and grounded as a generational workshop.

The visual style unites **tactile heritage craft** with **contemporary editorial minimalism**:
- **Atmosphere:** Warm, nostalgic, artisanal, and authentic without feeling dated or cluttered.
- **Audience:** Discerning collectors, ritual enthusiasts, and modern gift-seekers looking for genuine provenance, artisanal mastery, and trustworthy commerce.
- **Core Aesthetic Pillars:**
  - *Linen & Leather Foundations:* Soft parchment-tinted surfaces combined with deep saddlery accents establish immediate physical resonance.
  - *Quiet Precision:* Clean sans-serif utility pairs with heritage typography to guide shoppers effortlessly through origin stories, material guides, and transaction steps.
  - *Provenance Signifiers:* Subtle vintage guild stamps, brass-accented status pills, and understated certificate frames reassure users of 50 years of uninterrupted dedication.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the natural materials of the atelier: cured leather, sun-dried yerba leaves, polished brass accents, and unbleached parchment.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary (`#6E3B21` - Terracotta & Saddle Leather):** Anchors major conversion actions, active product filters, selected interactive states, and key navigational headers.
- **Secondary (`#4A5D43` - Yerba Leaf Olive):** Communicates botanical authenticity, organic provenance badges, inventory availability, and botanical note markers in fragrance accords.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#C89D56` - Aged Brass / Amber Gold):** Reserved for heritage anniversary marks, star ratings, editorial highlights, bespoke engraving indicators, and artisan signature trims.
- **Neutral Base & Surfaces:**
  - *Page Canvas:* `#FAF7F2` (Warm Linen Cream), creating a low-glare, tactile background that avoids sterile digital white.
  - *Surface Containers & Cards:* `#FFFFFF` (Parchment White) to give products clean photographic separation and high contrast.
  - *Borders & Dividers:* `#EBE4D8` (Milled Calico), delivering subtle structural lines without hard chromatic breaks.
- **Typography & Text (`#28221E` - Espresso Charcoal):**
  - High-contrast, deeply legible dark tone for body text, prices, specifications, and navigational labels, eliminating harshness while maintaining WCAG AAA accessibility across light surfaces.
  - *Muted Text (`#685E57`):* Secondary specifications, care instructions, and breadcrumbs.

## Typography

The typographic pairing reflects the store’s dual soul: timeless craft storytelling and swift modern utility.

- **Headlines & Display (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the narrative weight of a 50-year-old studio. Its graceful, high-contrast serif terminals evoke traditional stamped copper plates, hand-bound leather ledgers, and bespoke perfumery labels. It is used for hero banners, collection narratives, item names, and artisan quotes.
- **Interface & Content (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** A friendly, impeccably structured humanist sans-serif providing crystal-clear readability for product dimensions, pricing, olfactory pyramids, filtering chips, checkout flows, and trust disclosures.
- **Stylistic Treatment:** Headline text should avoid excessive uppercase styling, favoring natural title case that conveys warmth. Uppercase is strictly preserved for small brass-styled badges, product category pre-headers, and technical labels (`label-sm`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to balance generous editorial breathing room with dense, practical commercial browsing.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 3rem`, constrained to a maximum content container of 1320px for high-density catalog views and 1080px for long-form artisan workshop stories.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 2rem`. Product grids collapse smoothly from 4 columns to 2 columns with persistent filter drawering.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`. Elements span full grid widths; horizontal scroll carousels with visible peek cues are used for curated discovery lists and customer praise.

### Spacing Rhythm
- **Rhythm Model:** 4px base increment. Spacing follows functional groupings:
  - `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) regulate internal chip and button paddings.
  - `space-md` (16px) standardizes gap distances between card headers, pricing modules, and badge stacks.
  - `space-lg` (24px) dictates outer container padding within cards and modal frames.
  - `space-xl` (40px) orchestrates breathing room between distinct visual sections on the canvas.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **warm tonal layering** and **soft ambient shadows**, eschewing cold industrial drop shadows in favor of ambient lighting that mimics sunlight falling across a polished wooden bench.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Canvas (`#FAF7F2`):** The grounded base plane. Zero elevation.
2. **Subtle Outlines:** Primary surfaces rely on a 1px boundary of `#EBE4D8` rather than heavy shadows to establish quiet boundaries.
3. **Card Rest Elevation:** Product cards sit on pure `#FFFFFF` resting atop the linen canvas with a gentle ambient drop:
   `box-shadow: 0px 2px 8px rgba(40, 34, 30, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px rgba(40, 34, 30, 0.02);`
4. **Hover & Interactive State:** When hovering over interactive products, cards elevate subtly with a terracotta-warmed contact shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0px 8px 24px rgba(110, 59, 33, 0.08), 0px 2px 6px rgba(40, 34, 30, 0.04); transform: translateY(-2px);`
5. **Overlays & Drawers (Cart, Filters):** Modals and flyout carts utilize a deeper grounding shadow alongside a 40% warm espresso backdrop mask:
   `box-shadow: -4px 0px 32px rgba(40, 34, 30, 0.16);`

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **soft, tailor-crafted radius (Level 1)**. This mirrors the subtle softening of aged leather edges, hand-hammered alpaca rims, and bevelled perfume glass, maintaining structural architectural discipline without harsh rectangular sharpness.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to form inputs, structural data cards, image containers, and table layouts.
- **Medium Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to product cards, modal containers, and checkout summaries.
- **Pill Radius (Full `9999px`):** Reserved specifically for badges, trust seals, product tags (e.g., "Calabaza Seleccionada", "50 Años"), search inputs, and primary action buttons, providing an inviting, tactile contrast to the rectangular grid of product cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart, Secure Checkout):** Full pill radius, solid `#6E3B21` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `label-md` typography. Hover shifts to `#5C321E` with a subtle 2px vertical lift.
- **Secondary Action (View Craftsmanship, Secondary Triggers):** Full pill radius, transparent background with 1.5px border in `#4A5D43`, text in `#4A5D43`. Hover transitions to a soft green wash (`rgba(74, 93, 67, 0.08)`).
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** Unboxed, bolded `#6E3B21` with a fine amber underline (`#C89D56`) that expands on hover.

### Trust Badges & Chips
- **Anniversary & Provenance Seals:** Pill-shaped, composed with a warm linen background (`#F5EFE6`), fine border in `#C89D56`, `#28221E` text, accompanied by a small brass star or wreath icon.
- **Filter Chips:** Pill-shaped, neutral state has a 1px border of `#EBE4D8`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#28221E`. Active state shifts to `#4A5D43` background with white text, providing distinct botanical confirmation.

### Product Cards
- Built on a crisp `#FFFFFF` container with a 1px border of `#EBE4D8`.
- Image area uses a soft 4:5 portrait ratio with a warm off-white canvas backdrop, ensuring natural gourd textures, polished silver reflections, and glass flacons stand out.
- Header showcases the category pre-header in `label-sm` (`#C89D56`), product title in `headline-sm` serif, and price paired with stock status in `label-lg`.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid border `#EBE4D8`, corner radius 4px (`roundedness: 1`), padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- Focus state features a crisp 1.5px border of `#6E3B21` with zero generic blue glow, keeping the ritualistic, earthen tone intact.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Radio markers use concentric double rings in `#6E3B21`.
- Checkboxes maintain a 4px soft corner radius with `#6E3B21` fill and `#FFFFFF` hand-drawn-style checkmark.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Fragrance Accord & Yerba Profile Bar:** Horizontal sensory breakdown meters featuring a segmented track in `#EBE4D8` with progressive fills in `#4A5D43` (for herbal notes) and `#C89D56` (for amber/wood notes).
- **Bombilla Sizing Guide Modal:** Clean technical drawing overlay pairing alpaca metal specifications with appropriate gourd diameter recommendations.
- **Craftsmanship Authenticity Certificate:** A distinct surface framed with a double hairline border in `#EBE4D8` featuring the artisan workshop stamp and batch serial indicator.
